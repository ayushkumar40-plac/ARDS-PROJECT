About the Research Prototype

System architecture, biomechanical scoring formulation, and regulatory disclaimers.

Medical & Regulatory Prototype Disclaimer
  • Software-Only Research Prototype: The Adaptive Rehabilitation Decision Support System is an academic and engineering prototype. It is NOT an FDA/CE-cleared medical device.
  • Clinical Decision Support Only: This system does not replace the professional clinical diagnosis, physical assessment, or treatment judgment of certified physiotherapists, prosthetists, or orthopedic physicians.
  • Synthetic & Simulated Telemetry: Data displayed in this prototype may contain synthetic, simulated, or anonymized biomechanical datasets designed to demonstrate system behaviors and safety governor algorithms.

Rehabilitation Composite Scoring Formula

The Rehabilitation Score ($0–100$) integrates 5 core biomechanical domains weighted by clinical recovery priority:

Score = 0.30×GaitVelocity + 0.25×Stability + 0.20×ForceControl + 0.15×Symmetry + 0.10×(100 - Fatigue)
0 – 40: Poor

High physical assistance needed; significant gait deficit.

41 – 60: Moderate

Supervised training protocol; guarded load progression.

61 – 80: Improving

Positive adaptation; graduated resistance loading.

81 – 100: Good

High functional autonomy; community ambulation ready.

Modular System Architecture

The system follows a modular 4-tier decoupled pipeline:

1. Sensing & Ingestion

Wearable IMUs, socket capacitive pressure arrays, and force-sensing insoles.

2. ML Inference Engine

Ensemble Random Forest & SHAP surrogate for spatiotemporal gait classification.

3. Safety Governor

Deterministic rule bounds clamping progression to protect stump tissue integrity.

4. Human-in-the-Loop

Interactive clinician dashboard, alert triage, and printable clinical sign-offs.
