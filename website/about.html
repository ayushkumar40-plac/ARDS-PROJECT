About ARDS

We are a multidisciplinary team of clinicians, engineers, and researchers building the future of intelligent rehabilitation care — where every decision is data-driven, transparent, and safe.

Our Mission

To empower clinicians with explainable, real-time AI decision support that improves rehabilitation outcomes for lower-limb amputee patients worldwide.

We believe technology should amplify clinical expertise — not replace it. ARDS combines predictive analytics with a transparent safety governor, ensuring every recommendation is both intelligent and accountable.
