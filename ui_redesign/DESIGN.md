## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a compassionate, warm, clinical yet human mobile experience tailored for individuals navigating amputee rehabilitation and recovery. It is intentionally designed to alleviate the fatigue, uncertainty, and cognitive overload associated with complex medical telemetry and biomechanical data.

### Design Movement
The interface blends **Minimalist Care** with **Tactile Empathy**:
- Generous breathing space and warm neutral surfaces (avoiding stark, sterile hospital whites or cold, detached tech palettes).
- Gentle depth via soft tonal layering, rounded pill signifiers, and supportive physical affordances.
- High-contrast typography paired with soft-edged containers that evoke safety, trust, stability, and human connection.

### Core Tenets
1. **Dignity & Autonomy:** Plain-language explanations accompany all sensor readouts, replacing alarming clinical jargon with clear next steps.
2. **Effortless Accessibility:** WCAG AAA contrast standard for all functional copy, wide finger targets (minimum 48px), and gentle visual cues.
3. **Safety Through Clarity:** Non-punitive traffic light feedback (Sage, Amber, Rose) prioritizes pacing, restorative rest, and daily balance.

## Colors

The color palette centers on restorative, non-threatening tones that bridge clinical confidence and therapeutic warmth.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#0D7A82` - Warm Calming Teal):** Anchor color for primary actions, active navigational indicators, and affirmative guidance.
- **Secondary (`#1E3A5F` - Deep Ocean Blue):** Grounding tone for key headers, strong boundaries, and institutional credibility.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` - Sky Blue Accent):** Informational highlights, soft progress highlights, and interactive accents.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` - Soothing Warm White/Slate-50):** Screen background canvas that eliminates glare while maintaining high contrast.

### Traffic Light Status System
Engineered for emotional safety and cognitive clarity without anxiety-inducing stark alerts:
- **Doing Great / Safe (`#2E7D32` on `#E8F5E9`):** Grounded soft sage green indicating balanced movement, acceptable socket pressure, and target gait patterns.
- **Heads Up / Balance Watch (`#B45309` on `#FEF3C7`):** Warm golden honey tone prompting mindfulness, posture correction, or scheduled hydration.
- **Needs Attention / Rest Needed (`#BE123C` on `#FFE4E6`):** Soft rose/coral signaling imperative rest, socket release, or fatigue boundaries rather than failure.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly curved geometry, open apertures, and exceptional legibility across impaired vision or motion-induced reading contexts.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Base Body Copy:** Set at `16px` minimum with a generous `1.56` (`25px`) line-height to reduce visual crowding and cognitive strain.
- **Section Headers:** Bold, scannable weights (`700` and `800`) allow users in physical therapy sessions to absorb section purposes at a glance.
- **Microcopy & Explanations:** Explainer tooltips, badges, and assistive captions never scale below `12px`, maintaining strict WCAG AAA conformance against their container surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for mobile-first handheld use during walking sessions, wheelchair transfers, or seated rest periods.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Fluid Single-Column Canvas:** Handheld views utilize a vertical fluid column bounded by `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer canvas margins to prevent accidental palm edge activation.
- **Touch-First Rhythm:** Components align along a `4px`/`8px` baseline grid. Vertical spacing between independent modules (`space-lg` = `24px`) provides restful whitespace between clinical charts.
- **Target Safety Zones:** High-priority execution triggers (e.g., "Start Session", "Emergency Pause", "I Need Rest") live within the lower two-thirds thumb reach zone of mobile displays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through gentle atmospheric elevation and tonal contrast rather than high-contrast structural borders, fostering a soothing and supportive demeanor.

### Elevation Levels
- **Canvas (Level 0):** Background set to `#F8FAFC`, presenting zero elevation.
- **Resting Card (Level 1):** Main data containers utilize `#FFFFFF` layered over the canvas, supported by an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with a hairline stroke `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (Level 2):** Bottom sheets, expanded accordion dialogs, and explainers use `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Interactive State Elevation:** Active touch states slightly depress downward rather than projecting forward, emphasizing tactile affirmation and stability.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects organic comfort, discarding aggressive corners in favor of protective, rounded contours.

### Shape Rationale
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Employ `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem` / `20px`) corner radiuses to reinforce a soft, approachable aesthetic.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Use full capsule rounding (`9999px`) for badges, filters, tags, and progress tracker endpoints.
- **Inputs & Sliders:** Feature `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`) geometries, providing comfortable visual targets for motor-impaired taps.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Filled `#0D7A82` with `#FFFFFF` label text, `rounded-xl` corners, and minimum dimensions of `52px` height to exceed the 48px touch target rule.
- **Secondary / Rest Action:** `#E8F5E9` container with `#2E7D32` bold label for restorative pause prompts.
- **Tactile Feedback:** Tap states scale subtly (`0.98`) with clear focus rings (`3px solid rgba(13, 122, 130, 0.4)`).

### Status Pills & Explainer Badges
- **Structure:** Encapsulated capsule badges with leading soft emoji/icon slot followed by scannable label.
- **"What this means" Badges:** Subtle sky-blue accents (`#F0F9FF` background, `#0369A1` text) featuring an informational icon that expands bottom drawers with plain-language explanations.

### Progress Gauges & Visual Boundaries
- **Biomechanical Arc / Bar:** Horizontal progress tracks (`12px` height, fully rounded) partitioned into distinct clinical safe zones (Soft Sage, Warm Amber, Calm Coral).
- **Safety Indicator Needle:** Rounded target needle indicator showing current metric relative to healthy normative thresholds.

### Expandable Accordion Details
- **Container:** Bordered by `1px solid #E2E8F0` with a smooth ease-in-out transition.
- **Plain-Language Header:** Clear query phrasing (e.g., *"Why is my fatigue score yellow today?"*).
- **Expanded Body:** Rich supportive narrative with action recommendations and direct link to connect with clinician.

### Form Inputs & Adjusters
- **Text & Telemetry Fields:** Minimum `50px` height, `#FFFFFF` fill with `#CBD5E1` border, transitioning to `#0D7A82` on focus.
- **Range Sliders:** Oversized touch thumbs (`28px` circular knob) with visible thumb drop shadow, preventing finger occlusion during adjustment.
